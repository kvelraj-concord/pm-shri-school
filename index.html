School Heritage & Journey

5 Decades of Educational Evolution

Documented transformation of PM SHRI Govt. High School Nowpora (NPKP) from a village primary institution to a nationally recognized PM SHRI benchmark.

Official Notice Board Record Click to Enlarge
70
Year 1970

Established as Primary School

Founded to extend primary literacy and education to children of Now Pora Kher Pora and adjacent rural hamlets in South Kashmir.

05
Year 2005

Upgraded to Upper Primary Level

Promoted to Middle School standard to expand enrolment, ensure student retention, and provide middle-wing science and mathematics teaching.

19
Year 2019

Secondary High School Status

Elevated to a Government High School, introducing 9th and 10th grade curricula affiliated with JKBOSE, establishing dedicated lab spaces and faculty.

Year 2022 • Phase One

Selected under PM SHRI Scheme

Handpicked in Phase One of the national PM SHRI program to serve as a model school equipped with 21st-century IT labs, vocational curricula, STEM equipment, and sustainable campus initiatives.
